02 Two endpoints, one shared Service

Four real Actions, and what they actually call. TransactionService and TransferService both end up at NotificationService — and TransferService writes straight to TransactionRepository without going through its sibling Service at all, because a transfer has to lock two wallets at once, which TransactionService::create() was never built to do.

Service → Repository, skipping a sibling Service

Actions and Services are not two names for the same thing here: an Action is a single, disposable entry point (one per __invoke, matching one Controller method); a Service is the thing more than one Action — or another Service — is allowed to call. The diagrams above are the actual dependency graph for the transaction/transfer corner of app/Actions and app/Services, not a stylized example.
